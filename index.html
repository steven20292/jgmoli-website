FAQ

Before you build
your cockpit.

These are the questions that matter most before a high-value cockpit purchase. Where final Australian specifications or policies are not yet approved, the answer is marked accordingly.

What is included in the listed cockpit price?

The current website price refers to the cockpit platform and model-specific hardware direction. PC, displays, audio and selected upgrade packages may be configured separately unless a final package states otherwise.

Do the cockpits include monitors or a gaming PC?

Not by default in the current website concept. Monitor and PC packages are intended to be configurable add-ons. Final Australian package inclusions are still subject to approval.

Which model should I choose?

PRO is the all-round multi-screen option, PLUS is the strongest ultrawide / visual statement option, and ULTRA is positioned as the flagship heavy-duty platform. Use the comparison table and cockpit finder to narrow the choice.

Will it fit in my room?

Room footprint is only part of the question. Door width, corridors, stairs, turning space and final installation clearance should all be checked before delivery. The current Fit & Access section is designed to support this assessment.

Can I use my existing ultrawide or triple-screen setup?

Potentially, yes. Current platform references include 49-inch ultrawide support, triple-screen support and selected multi-screen layouts depending on the model. Exact compatibility should be confirmed against your monitor size, VESA pattern and total bracket load.

Can I use a steering wheel and pedals for sim racing?

The website is being positioned for gaming and simulation use, including wheel-and-pedal builds. Final mounting compatibility depends on the chosen cockpit, mounting hardware and your specific peripherals.

Is delivery and installation included?

The current Australian ownership concept includes a delivery / assembly / setup pathway. Final delivery areas, installation scope, access requirements and any associated charges still require operational approval.

What warranty will MOLI provide in Australia?

Final MOLI Australia warranty terms are not yet confirmed. Supplier platform warranties vary by model, so the public website should only publish a final Australian warranty once management and operations approve the service model.

Is finance available?

The current plan models finance scenarios to show possible monthly payments, but finance partners, fees, eligibility and final terms are not yet confirmed. Any live finance offer will need approval before publication.

Can I see or try a cockpit before buying?

That is the intended purchase path. Customers can register interest or request a private demo as Australian demo opportunities become available.

Can the finish or configuration be customised?

Selected colours, materials, monitor layouts and hardware upgrades are part of the current product direction, but availability, lead time and pricing depend on the final supplier and MOLI configuration.

When can I place a final order online?

The current site is still in the product-validation stage. The next development phase is planned around a configurator, “Your Build” summary, cart and review-order flow once final product data and commercial terms are approved.
